## Official Documents and Guidelines

### Important Rules

- Notify the company within the specified time frame after an accident.
- Submit valid vehicle registration, road permit, driver license, and accident evidence.
- Claims may be rejected for drunk driving, use of prohibited substances, expired insurance, or non-compliant vehicle operation.
- Evidence-based assessment is required, and claims are processed under current rules.

### Claim Eligibility Basics

- Vehicle and driver documents must be valid during the claim period.
- Accident information must be registered within the required timeframe.
- Incident facts and evidence must be available for assessment.

### Common Claim Rejection Conditions

- Driving under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances
- Expired insurance or missing vehicle renewal
- Incomplete or missing documents
- False or misleading information
